About me

From law books to product launches.

I trained and worked as a lawyer in Argentina. Along the way I realised what I enjoyed most was building things — so I taught myself to design and code, and today I create my own products end to end.

I care about the whole journey: the idea, the interface, the code, the launch on the App Store and Google Play, and everything I learn from the people who use it. I live between Spanish and English, which is why so much of my work is about languages.
